# Code of Conduct

Kestrel is a place to learn about and build rocket-landing guidance
together. Everyone is welcome, whatever their background or level of
experience.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Rust,
  optimization or spaceflight.
- Critique ideas and code, never people. Explain the reason, and back
  technical claims with measurements where you can.
- Assume good intent, and accept correction gracefully.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests
that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly do. To report
a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their GitHub
profile.
